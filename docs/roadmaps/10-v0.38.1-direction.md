# A way forward from v0.38.1

October 1, 2026. This is a proposed product direction for New Drugs, based on the current production system, the founder's decisions, and the earlier [roadmap study](README.md). It replaces that study as the current decision map. It does not authorize outreach, paid experiments, production changes, or a new feature by itself.

## The decision

Make New Drugs a place where someone can find a person they want to know, have a welcome first exchange, and choose to come back. The public substance comes from people: their profiles, posts, replies, invitations and live conversations. The Agent helps a person find and act on what matters to them. Log keeps their lived record private.

An interesting public conversation is a complete outcome. So is a friendship that moves to a DM, a call, an in-person activity or another app. New Drugs does not need to capture every later interaction to have helped.

This is a narrower promise than “an AI that does everything socially.” It can be understood on a first visit and tested with the product that already exists.

## Why the product feels stalled

Production has four primary modes, public posts and search, Nearby and All people, Circle mutual context, invitations, DMs, video calls, Talk, private Log, automations, an inbox, and CLI/MCP parity. The software has acquired many ways to do something once people are present. It has not yet established a repeatable reason for a real group to show up together. An empty People, Posts or Talk view cannot supply that reason through better rendering alone.

The September roadmap pack explored plans, groups, discovery, local guides and agent follow-through. The founder declined its original bookmarks-to-plans-to-circles sequence. Since then, automations, Circle as a mutual-friends filter, video calls, Talk and much of the proposed infrastructure have shipped. Copying its old feature list into the next release would misstate the current product.

The personal-website trial is a separate warning. A draft preview is publicly reachable by link. Private Log material was mistakenly placed in one, then removed and the old link revoked. The founder stopped that project. Website polish is not a rescue strategy for the social app. The public/private boundary must be redesigned before another builder trial.

### Decisions already made

| Settled for now | Still open |
| --- | --- |
| Circle means a mutual-friends view, with context on People cards. It is not a group chat. | Which first group has a reason to use New Drugs together? |
| Profiles are human-authored, public posts are human acts, and Log remains private or explicitly shared. | Which repeat action is the first product promise: conversation, introductions, or coordination? |
| Nearby and All are both real discovery scopes; location is coarse. | Would human-curated entry materially help a newcomer? |
| Screen sharing is deferred, and the founder stopped the personal-site experiment. | Does a real group ask for group posting or a narrow plan after using today's tools? |
| Hosted AI is billed at cost; direct CLI/MCP use is free from New Drugs' side. | When does a stronger hosted model improve a chosen task enough to justify its latency and cost? |

An open question is a test or founder decision. It is not a hidden feature request.

## The human loop to prove

| Step | Current surface | The question to answer with real people |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrive | A link to a person, post or New Drugs itself | Do they understand why this particular place is worth opening? |
| Notice | Human-written Posts, People, Circle, Nearby/All search | Do they encounter someone or something they genuinely care about? |
| Approach | Reply or a human-written invitation | Does contact feel specific and welcome? |
| Continue | Accepted DM, video call, or public Talk | Is it easy to carry a good exchange forward? |
| Return | Another conversation, shared activity, or voluntary check-in | Does anyone choose another interaction when the founder stops prompting? |
| Remember | Private Log and optional Agent memory | Does the app help the person retain what they want without turning private life into public content? |

No one must traverse every step. Talk can be valuable without a friendship request. A useful post can end at reading. An offline friendship can continue elsewhere. The sequence is an observation tool, not a funnel to impose on users.

### What each mode is for

- **Posts:** the public conversation. Human-made text, links and images make people legible beyond profile labels. Search and selected-post lists help a person find actual source posts.
- **Friends:** people, accepted connections and Circle. Mutual friends are context, not an endorsement or a group-chat membership. Invitations stay personal and human-written.
- **Agent:** a private way to navigate the app and handle a chosen task. It should show exact people, posts and consequences, while the normal controls remain sufficient. CLI/MCP users get the same domain operations without paying New Drugs for hosted reasoning.
- **Log:** private or explicitly shared memories of things that happened. It can help the owner reflect and remember. It does not silently supply public profile or website content.

The current modes are enough to test the first promise. A fifth tab is not the next move.

## Work in order

### 1. Close the trust and reliability gap

This is a bounded preparation pass, not a general redesign. Run the existing journey with two real accounts on a desktop and a physical phone: arrive through a post/person link, find someone, send and accept an invitation, DM, start and answer a call, join/leave Talk, and return to the same view after refresh. Check notification delivery, mute state and the actual sound on devices. Fix failures that prevent a normal participant from doing that journey. Keep Log private and check that links and website previews disclose only their intended audience.

The recent website incident needs an explicit future design before builder work resumes: public-by-link preview semantics, a clear selection of public material, separate authorization for a private source to become public, and a way to revoke a preview. Prompt wording alone did not prevent the mistake. The current unpublished trial is not part of the first community launch.

Make the hosted Agent's instructions describe the native DM call and current Talk behavior. They still mention an external pair.video link in one place. Evaluate a stronger optional model for difficult creation tasks with actual quality and cost evidence; do not change every social chat turn because one builder task disappointed us.

For that evaluation, use a small set of real, owner-approved tasks: finding source-backed people/posts, planning a concrete next step, editing a complex artifact, and carrying out a reviewed social action. Compare usefulness, factual grounding, tone, latency and actual provider receipts. Let the person choose a more expensive mode for work that earns it. Keep direct CLI/MCP operations model-independent.

### 2. Bring one real community through the current product

The founder chooses one reachable group with a reason to know one another. A local cluster with some existing connections is useful because Circle and nearby discovery can show real context. The [earlier pilot plan](06-pilots-measurement-and-funding.md) suggests roughly 12 to 25 willing adults for two to three weeks as an operating size, not a network-effect threshold. Start smaller if support capacity or willingness is lower.

Invite through actual human relationships. Let participants write their own profiles and posts, or decline to post. Give them more than one valid first action: read, reply, browse people, send an invitation, join a live Talk, or use the Agent. Do not stage accounts, generate a feed, require a profile essay, or force an offline meeting.

During the first days, the founder can help people when they get stuck. Then reduce that help deliberately. The key observation is what people choose when the founder is no longer carrying each interaction.

### 3. Build one complete slice from the obstacle observed

| Repeated observation | Next product response worth testing |
| --- | --- |
| People arrive through a friend but cannot find the relevant people or posts | A human-curated, shareable entry into selected public people/posts for that community. Test manual links first. Do not turn this into people bookmarks or a generated popularity ranking. |
| People find interesting material but hesitate to contact someone | Improve the invitation and reply context, audience clarity and delivery. Observe the human wording and response, not only a click rate. |
| Conversations start but changing a time/place repeatedly causes confusion | A small plan record with time, place, audience, response, change and cancellation. Build its complete lifecycle and manual controls before more planning features. |
| People repeatedly use Talk but cannot find one another at the right time | A narrow way to announce a planned Talk and return to it. Keep live Talk separate from Log's record of actual hangouts. |
| A few people want to post together | Revisit group posting with explicit membership, visibility and moderation rules. This is a later decision. Circle remains the mutual-friends filter; group chat was declined. |
| People enjoy a few exchanges but do not voluntarily return | Revisit the community, invitation and value proposition before adding another capability. Ask participants who stopped as well as those who stayed. |

The first implementation after the pilot should solve one observed failure end to end in the UI, agent and shared operations. The project should have an owner, a stop condition and a way to remove it if it does not help.

## Conditional longer arc

**One community:** the near-term proof is a small set of people who recognize why they are here and choose to interact again. Product craft goes into the post/person/invitation/DM path, direct links and trustworthy mobile behavior. An Agent task is useful when it saves a real step. This stage does not need another primary mode.

**Several communities:** if distinct groups sustain their own conversation without the founder hosting each exchange, New Drugs can support human-curated entry points and, if requested, group posting with clear audience rules. Local community hosts would need bounded moderation tools and visible responsibility. Talk and Circle may help people cross between communities, but do not silently reveal membership or private relationships.

**A service people rely on:** when people actually depend on New Drugs, account portability, reliable restore, operational succession, independent clients and a more explicit shared-cost model become product requirements. The CLI/MCP contract is a starting point for user control; it is not yet a promise of federation or self-hosting. The [further-out experiments](07-longer-horizon-experiments.md) are options to test at that stage.

The move between stages is earned by repeat human use, trust and operating evidence, not a calendar date or a version number.

## Evidence that would change the decision

Keep the review small and human-readable. Record, with consent and minimal identifiers:

- whether a person reached a **meaningful first action** they chose, such as a useful reply, a relevant person, a welcome invitation or a live conversation;
- whether they chose **another interaction** with someone, on or off the app, after the first one;
- where **coordination or trust** failed, using a participant's description rather than a guessed event score;
- whether an action reached the intended person once, at the right time, with a usable failure path;
- founder support time, moderation burden and actual hosted AI/media/search cost.

Do not collect private Log or DM text, precise movement, background microphone data or inferred personality traits for this review. Avoid tiny breakdowns that reveal an individual. Page views and message totals are diagnostic only. More time in the app is not the north star.

A good decision review can conclude that public conversation is the product, that a narrow coordination object is needed, or that the chosen community has no reason to use this product yet. Those conclusions lead to different builds. The founder should choose which one to pursue after seeing the evidence.

## Operating envelope

The ten defects in the original [scaling audit](../SCALING_AUDIT.md) were repaired. The current system has bounded Log/history work, Qdrant-backed retrieval, split worker processes, object storage and targeted live events. The old 128-live-connection and 10,000-document statements in September roadmap files are historical. They are not current capacity limits.

Dev and production still share one 4 GB / 2 vCPU host with Mongo, search, workers and LiveKit. It is an early-community operating setup, without a measured large-corpus or concurrent-media envelope. The current `/api/health` checks the database, so a launch review also needs queue age, search freshness, media join success and external dependency failures. Run load experiments on isolated infrastructure, never by stressing the shared production host. Keep hosted AI charges, platform-funded search/media costs and voluntary support distinct. Backups remain deferred by the founder until the service has traction.

## Held work

Screen sharing in Talk remains saved as a deferred item. Personal website creation is paused after the failed trial. A native app, group posting, public Log-derived identity, autonomous social outreach, paid recurring reports, federation and broad community governance are separate decisions. None is required to learn whether the current human loop has value.

## Why this is an original New Drugs direction

Adjacent services show useful pieces. [Bluesky's starter packs](https://bsky.social/about/blog/06-26-2024-starter-packs) show that a person can bring someone into a curated slice of a network. [Bumble's current BFF description](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/30781191036317-Changes-to-Bumble-For-Friends-and-Bumble-BFF-Mode) includes one-to-one and group friendship. [Partiful's time poll](https://help.partiful.com/en-us/articles/15525422-can-i-poll-or-survey-my-guests) shows one concrete coordination step. None establishes that New Drugs should copy its full product.

New Drugs' combination is already unusual: human-authored local conversation, friend-of-friend context, direct voice/video, a private record of life and an agent that can operate the same social system. Its next proof is whether people want the human part enough to use it again.
