# New Drugs after v0.38.1

October 1, 2026. A proposed product and build direction. New Drugs is less than a week old. Its current audience says almost nothing about product demand, so this document does not diagnose a retention problem or make recruiting a prerequisite for design work. It supersedes the September [possibility study](README.md) as the current proposal. It is not authorization to release code, contact people or publish content.

## The product to build

**Find people through what they actually say and make.** A person can enter through a human-written post, a profile, a friend of a friend, a live Talk, or a request to the Agent. New Drugs should show the real source and let the person decide whether to read, reply, invite, talk or leave it alone. The system should make contact easier without manufacturing identity or urgency.

That gives the shipped modes distinct jobs:

| Mode | Job |
| --- | --- |
| Posts | People show what they care about in their own words, photos and links. Conversation is valuable even when it stays online. |
| Friends | Find people through public expression, coarse area and real mutual friends. A human controls every invitation. |
| Agent | Search and connect the existing surfaces for a chosen task. Show evidence and exact records, then use the same ordinary operations and review rules. |
| Log | Keep the person's record of actual life private or explicitly shared with named attendees. It can help the owner remember without supplying a public persona. |

Talk and accepted-friend video calls give those people immediate ways to spend time together. CLI/MCP lets a person use their own agent as another client of the same product. A good public exchange, a DM, a call, an actual hangout and a connection that continues elsewhere are all valid outcomes.

The unusual part is the combination, not a fifth mode: human expression, source-backed discovery, mutual context, live conversation, private memory and an agent with real but bounded authority.

## What v0.38.1 already provides

Posts/replies have public semantic indexing. Friends has Nearby, All and Circle, with materialized mutual names and photos. Search can retrieve profiles, posts and replies through separate paths. The Agent can curate an ordered post list and reopen it. Invitations, DMs, native calls and public Talk exist. Log, private search, memory, automations and the Agent inbox exist. These are implementation assets, not a backlog of missing foundations.

The interfaces between them are younger than the individual features. In particular, [Friends search currently asks for profile results](../../server/operations.ts) while public posts and replies have a separate index and richer evidence. A person can write the most revealing post on the app and still be hard to discover as a person through that writing. That is a product gap worth building now, before a traffic study can say anything useful.

## Recommended next release: People through public voice

Use the existing Friends search control. A query such as “people who make little games” should return discoverable people whose own public profile, post or reply supports the result. Each person appears once. A result card carries the normal name, photo, coarse area, mutual context and Friend/Hide controls, plus one or two exact human-written source snippets when those explain the match. The source opens the post or reply and Back returns to the same People results.

The card does not assert that the person is available, compatible, expert in a topic or interested in the searcher. It says what they wrote. The person chooses whether to contact them. Empty-query Nearby, All and Circle browsing keep their present behavior. A semantic query does not silently widen a strict Nearby or Circle scope.

Implementation should use a shared read operation, tentatively `people.discover`, for UI, CLI, MCP and the hosted Agent. Reuse the current public profile/post/reply indexes and author IDs. Retrieve a bounded candidate set, group by author, combine lexical/semantic evidence, then reauthorize both the person and each source before returning it. Blocks, hides, suspensions, deleted/moderated posts and changing visibility must win over a cached vector or selection. Circle uses its maintained pair index; this request does not walk the friend graph. Prove the candidate and unique-author work at an explicit test envelope from the [scaling audit](../SCALING_AUDIT.md).

The Agent can request and refine the same results, introduce them briefly, and open the native People view. It should not paste a wall of invented summaries into chat. Preserve real source links and the user's ordinary controls. This is a direct expression of the Twitter plus BFF premise and a meaningful use of search that is already paid for.

**Done means:** a real public post can make its author discoverable under the correct scope; an unrelated or newly inaccessible post cannot; a result explains itself with an exact link; Friend, Hide, Back and mobile layout behave normally; the same operation works from CLI/MCP; and bounded work is measured. This is one complete product slice, not a new search page.

## Build outward from that slice

### Source-backed Agent results

Extend the existing selected-post behavior into a typed native selection for people, posts and live Talks. Let the Agent order a small set of current records, attach its reason to exact human-written evidence, and let the person refine the selection in the existing Friends or Posts panel. Reopen a selection by link. Reauthorize every ID when the view opens, because a post can be deleted or a connection can block access after selection. No compatibility percentages, inferred profile traits or people bookmarks are needed.

This work can follow or partly share the People-discovery implementation. It should not introduce a sixth search design or an agent-only way to perform a basic social action.

### Give Talk a before and after

Talk currently centers on live rooms. A host could author an upcoming Talk with a title, optional description, start time and stable share link. A simple card appears under Posts before it starts; the host starts it, and that card becomes the existing live Talk. Someone can choose a reminder. A human may post a follow-up link afterward. This is a public-program object, not a group chat, recording system or automatic Log entry. The host's title remains required and human-written.

First complete the open two-person and physical-phone media checks in the [calling reference](../communications/PAIR_VIDEO_REFERENCE.md). Scheduling a room does not help if people cannot join or hear it reliably.

### Circle introductions, later

Circle already shows named mutual friends. A later explicit action could ask **one chosen mutual friend** to introduce two people. The mutual can decline or forward a human-written request; the recipient decides whether to connect. Only an accepted one-to-one connection opens Messages. There is no trust score or automatic endorsement. Each transition must recheck the live friendships, block/hide/suspension state and recipient authority; materialized mutual counts alone may lag. Circle was just accepted as done for now, so this is a next chapter, not a correction to the current release.

## Agent depth as a separate track

The hosted Agent is currently configured for GPT-6 Luna at medium reasoning. The founder has observed that it is weak at some complex creative work. Evaluate an opt-in stronger path for demanding tasks with a visible per-run cost ceiling, separate session/spec identity and actual usage receipts. Compare it on source-backed social discovery, a complex artifact and a reviewed action. Ordinary chat and model-free CLI/MCP operations need not become more expensive to improve a hard task.

Correct stale hosted instructions that still suggest an external pair.video link where the native DM call now exists. For any public-output task, the Agent should receive public records or the specific private material the person chose to expose. A request to use private Log as design context is not consent to put Log content in a publicly reachable website draft. The recent preview mistake is a product trust failure to close before personal-site building resumes; prompt text alone did not prevent it.

## More distant possibilities

- **Human-made local trails or short show-and-tells:** someone deliberately publishes a few public places, links or things they made; others can read, talk or join. Do not derive the trail from anyone's device movement or private Log.
- **Group posting:** consider it only with a clear audience, membership and moderation model. The founder rejected group chats. Circle remains the mutual-friends filter.
- **A small plan record:** revisit only if the founder chooses coordination as a product direction. The previous bookmarks-to-plans-to-circles sequence was declined; it is not the next build by default.
- **Agent-to-agent coordination:** possible after individual authority and review are strong. A useful first object would be a structured proposal that two humans explicitly approve, not automatic outreach or an exchange of private calendars and diaries.
- **Portability and stewardship:** useful exports, documented external operations, recoverable service operation and independent clients can mature as people come to rely on the app. Federation or a native mobile app needs its own case.

Talk screen sharing stays in its saved deferred note. The personal-site experiment remains stopped and unpublished. These possibilities do not form a release schedule.

## Engineering and product constraints

The ten original scaling defects were repaired; old 128-connection and 10,000-document figures in September roadmap files are historical. Capacity under large public search corpora and concurrent real media remains unproven. Dev and production share a 4 GB / 2 vCPU host. Measure retrieval work, search freshness, media joins, worker queue age and cost before claiming growth capacity. Run stress work away from shared production.

The public/private boundary is part of the product model. Human-authored profiles stay human-authored. Private Log, chat, files and memory do not enter public discovery or site source because an Agent happened to read them. Agent suggestions link to current human records and use review for serious social actions. Background work is scoped and observable. New Drugs takes no cut from hosted AI; platform search and hosting remain separate shared costs. Backups remain deferred under the founder's current decision until the service has traction.

## How to choose the following build

This document recommends **People through public voice** as the next concrete project. Its behavior and safeguards are specified in the companion [build sequence](11-build-sequence.md). Once it is working, the founder can choose the next depth: better Agent presentation, a public Talk program, or mutual introductions. Real use will eventually refine that choice, but a week-old application's current audience is not evidence against developing the product now.

For comparison only, [Bluesky's starter packs](https://bsky.social/about/blog/06-26-2024-starter-packs) demonstrate human-curated entry into a social context, while [Bumble's current BFF description](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/30781191036317-Changes-to-Bumble-For-Friends-and-Bumble-BFF-Mode) spans one-to-one and group friendship. Neither determines New Drugs' roadmap. New Drugs can make its own discovery path from the human-written material and consent rules it already has.
