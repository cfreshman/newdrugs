These are three different ways Codex could develop the product from its shipped state. They are mutually comparable sketches, not three parallel work queues. Milestones have no promised date or version. The founder may choose, combine, reject or replace them.

## A. A more expressive public internet

**Product idea:** New Drugs becomes a place where people put original, human-made things into public conversation. The app gives those things good routes into a live exchange without asking the Agent to invent personality or content.

| Order if chosen | Concrete slice | Why this order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | Optional human-written photo descriptions, then a Post image made in Square from a blank canvas or newly chosen image | Improves public authorship and accessibility using existing Post and editor foundations. The ordinary composer stays quick. |
| A2 | One host-chosen public Post as an anchor in an already live Talk | Gives a chosen piece of public work a voice conversation, using the existing room and exact link. No scheduling or new Talk type. |
| A3 | Consider explicit coauthored or group-audience Posts | Shared authorship and membership add consent/history/moderation obligations, so they follow the simpler author-to-room path. |

The first decision is whether people should be offered a richer making tool inside Posts at all. The [media proposal](02-human-made-posts.md) and [Talk proposal](01-post-to-talk.md) can also stand alone. This road fails if editing machinery crowds out ordinary human posting or if the live anchor is rarely useful.

## B. A practical local internet

**Product idea:** Public material helps someone discover a place, offer, question or activity worth acting on. Its author controls the claims and whether the offer is still open. The app's approximate area makes local context useful without tracking a person.

| Order if chosen | Concrete slice | Why this order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B1 | Optional open/fulfilled/closed status on a human-authored offer or request Post | Tests whether a small state makes public asks more honest while retaining the normal post and reply model. |
| B2 | One human-curated trail through deliberately public places or links | Adds a distinct form only after ordinary local Posts show that authors want to curate something longer. |
| B3 | Consider richer coordination only after a separate founder decision | The prior plans-first sequence was declined. A status or trail does not quietly become an RSVP/event platform. |

The [local exchange proposal](03-local-exchange.md) details safety and search behavior. This road fails if the product becomes an unmaintained directory or people feel obliged to provide free labor. The agent may check external place facts with sources, but it does not invent a person's recommendation.

## C. A private companion with an open control plane

**Product idea:** New Drugs is valuable to its owner as a private record and a set of capabilities they can use with their preferred Agent, while the public network remains a separate, chosen surface.

| Order if chosen | Concrete slice | Why this order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | Make the audience of a Log invite code visible to its sharer; optionally protect interrupted private text drafts | Starts with trust and continuity in already used private flows. |
| C2 | Owner-requested, source-linked Log reflection over a chosen subset | Builds on shipped search/related operations without launching paid background analysis. |
| C3 | Narrow expiring external-agent grants for selected records, then deliberate export | Grants and portability become understandable after audience and ownership rules are explicit. |

The [private-continuity](04-private-continuity.md) and [open-agency](05-open-agency.md) proposals keep source access, media and revocation separate. This road fails if reflection adds generic commentary or if fine-grained grants are too confusing to trust. It must never use private Log to auto-fill public identity.

## Shared prerequisites and boundaries

Any selected slice should keep a normal manual path, the shared operation contract where useful, exact authorization on old links and source records, current mobile geometry, and an observable rollback or removal path. Production still needs the founder's explicit release request. Current physical-phone and two-person media checks are useful execution work, not evidence that a week-old product has failed.

Circle mutuals, live Talk, public cross-category `search.query` as an Agent tool, private Log, human-authored profiles and the stopped personal-website experiment remain as the founder specified. None of the three sketches revives the rejected global search tab, Circle introduction flow, scheduled Talk, model-upgrade roadmap, or group chat.
